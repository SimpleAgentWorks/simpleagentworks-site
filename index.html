What the base website includes

Designed and developed

A cinematic website developed for your business. You can supply images and copy, or we can generate them with your review.

Basic hosting

Basic site hosting is included. We host it on your behalf, subject to agreed usage limits. A web app or custom integrations need a separate quote.

Two edit rounds

Two rounds of edits before the initial site goes live. Typical build time is 3-10 business days after we have all the information and assets we need; timing is confirmed with each project.

Your business owns its URL and pays the domain registration cost. If we buy the domain on your behalf, its cost is passed through as an additional charge after you approve it. We agree on the final page count, hosting limits and ongoing support in the project scope.

How we start

1. Fit call

Tell us about your business, your current site, and the outcome you want.

2. Scope and assets

Agree on the work. Send your copy, photos and domain details, or ask us to generate materials for your review.

3. Build and review

We build the site, take two pre-launch edit rounds, and prepare the site to go live.

Need more than a website?

Booking flows, AI-assisted proposals, intranets and custom web apps can be scoped separately. They are not included in the base plan. Google Business Profile is free; our paid work is website design and implementation.

Questions

Do I own the domain? Yes. You buy and own it, or approve a separate pass-through purchase on your behalf.

When can the site launch? A basic site typically takes 3-10 business days after we have complete customer data, subject to final agreed scope.

How do I pay? After we agree on scope and terms, you can pay by Zelle or credit card. Payment details are shared during onboarding.
